Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how CodeSage helps you learn.

Does CodeSage give me the solution?

CodeSage uses progressive hints to help you solve problems yourself. The goal is understanding, not simply getting the answer.

How is CodeSage different from ChatGPT?

CodeSage is designed around structured programming practice, hint-first guidance, mistake tracking, and long-term learning.

Can I use CodeSage for DSA?

Yes. CodeSage is designed to help with algorithms, complexity, debugging, and programming problem solving.

What happens if I still can't solve the problem?

CodeSage can progressively increase the level of guidance instead of immediately revealing the solution.
